# Delivery and Payment

### How you get your order

We offer two ways to receive your order:

- **Delivery:** We bring your order straight to your door (please check our delivery areas).
- **Pickup:** You can also pick up your order from us (1 Sample Street, 12345 Sampletown).

### Payment options

- **On delivery:** Payment in **cash** only.
- **On pickup:** You can pay in cash or by **debit card**.

### Delivery areas

We deliver to the following areas only:

- **12345 Sampletown** (Sampletown and Nearby)
- **12346 Example Village**

### Delivery costs

- **Orders of €15.00 or more:** free delivery.
- **Orders under €15.00:** we charge a delivery fee of **€2.00**.

### Delivery time

Delivery usually takes about **30 minutes**. At busy times it may take a little longer.
